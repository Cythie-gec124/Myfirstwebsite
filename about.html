About Me

A little page about me ♡

hello, welcome!

Hello, and again, welcome to A Gentle Chronicle! I'm Dexie, a senior nursing student, navigating my
way through a bachelor's degree in nursing. Nursing has never been easy, and it has challenged me at
every step, but along the way, I have learned so much from the course.

Most importantly, nursing has taught me that care isn't just delivered at the bedside. It can also be seen in the little things we create, communicate, and do for others.

Whether I'm managing patient care during hospital rotations or working on creative projects, accuracy, empathy, and neatness are some of the values I try to carry with me.
